2.0.3p rig evidence: the teeth pin pull (RSQ Grenade's Face(), ported). RIG ONLY, NOT HEADSET-CONFIRMED.
QuestZDoom 17.3 series rig (RSQ_UBQSeries/tools/rig17/run17.sh), IWAD doom2.wad, MAP01, BD22 class (not Purist), poses from the
rig's qz_hmd (default 0 1.6 0) / qz_rhand / qz_lhand, triggers qz_rtrigger / qz_ltrigger. Console scripts: tools/teeth/run_a.txt,
run_b.txt. A test-only probe pk3 (tools/teeth/probe, never shipped) prints a TTP line on every change of the grenade's state
(RSG_State: pin, teeth, ring kept, spoon; GrenadeAmmo; HandGrenade actors in the world), and every tic while the trigger is held
with ttp_trace 1. face = the drawn grenade's body centre to the eyes (m), as Face() measures it; faceTic = tics held at the face;
ready = the teeth may take a pin (RSQ's far-radius / trigger re-arm). Defaults: rsg_face 1, near 0.47 m, far 0.71 m, hold 12.

Load order (the owner's, full): 01 brutal22test6 -> 02 BD22_VoxelPack -> 03 BD22_VR-Reloaded_2.0.3p -> 04-07 XMVE ->
GunBonsai-0.10.6 -> every RSQ_UBQSeries/pk3/*.pk3 (RSQ_Grenade stands down: it sees RSG_Handler) -> probe.
Lines below: consecutive identical states folded (faceTic counts shown at the first and pin tic; full per-tic trace further down).
